# Learning Notes: Core Mathematical & Cryptographic Foundations

## 1. Merkle Trees & Cryptographic Audit Trails
- **Concept:** A binary tree of cryptographic hashes where every leaf node is labeled with the cryptographic hash of a data block (transaction or review) and every non-leaf node is labeled with the cryptographic hash of its child nodes' labels.
- **Why in TrustLedger:**
  - Efficient and secure verification of contents in large sets of records ($O(\log N)$ audit proofs).
  - Immutability: altering any single review or transaction changes the Merkle root, immediately exposing tampering.
  - Zero-Knowledge Inclusion Proof: A business can prove to Google or an auditor that a review was generated by a confirmed appointment without exposing customer personal identifiable information (PII).

## 2. Temporal Burst Detection: CUSUM & Sliding Window Z-Scores
- **Problem:** Coordinated review attacks typically happen in rapid succession compared to baseline organic customer arrival rates.
- **Formulas:**
  - Standardized Poisson / Normal Poisson approximation for daily velocity:
    $$Z_t = \frac{x_t - \mu}{\sigma}$$
  - Cumulative Sum Control Chart (CUSUM) for detecting small, persistent shifts in mean velocity:
    $$S_t^+ = \max(0, S_{t-1}^+ + x_t - (\mu + k))$$
  - Thresholding $\tau = h \cdot \sigma$ determines statistical alarms with bounded false alarm rates.

## 3. Lexical Shingling & Jaccard Similarity (MinHash Approximation)
- **Problem:** Review farms frequently reuse boilerplates or lightly spin customer reviews.
- **Mechanism:**
  - Convert text into set of $k$-shingles (character or word $n$-grams).
  - Jaccard similarity:
    $$J(A, B) = \frac{|A \cap B|}{|A \cup B|}$$
  - For $N$ reviews, calculating all pairs is $O(N^2)$; MinHash hashing reduces pairwise estimation complexity to $O(M \cdot N)$ where $M$ is the number of hash functions.

## 4. Probabilistic Record Linkage (Fuzzy Matching Engine)
- **Components:**
  - Name similarity via normalized Levenshtein / Jaro-Winkler distance $S_{name} \in [0, 1]$.
  - Temporal delta decay function $w_{\Delta t} = \exp(-\lambda \cdot |\Delta t|)$ where $\Delta t = t_{review} - t_{booking}$.
  - Service category semantic match score $S_{service} \in \{0, 0.5, 1.0\}$.
  - Weighted composite confidence score:
    $$C = w_1 S_{name} + w_2 w_{\Delta t} + w_3 S_{service}$$
  - Confidence tiers:
    - $C \ge 0.82 \implies$ **CONFIRMED**
    - $0.55 \le C < 0.82 \implies$ **PROBABLE**
    - $C < 0.55 \implies$ **UNVERIFIED**
